Privacy Policy

Tally Invoices · Effective 25 October 2026

The short version

  • Tally Invoices has no servers, no accounts and no database. Everything you enter — your business details, clients, invoices and prices — is stored only on your own device — and, if you turn on iCloud Sync, in your own private iCloud.
  • We do not collect, receive, sell or share any of your data. There are no analytics, no advertising, no cookies and no tracking.
  • If you choose to connect Gmail, Tally uses Google's permission only to send the invoice emails you ask it to send, directly from your device to Google. It cannot read your email.

1. About this app

Tally Invoices (“Tally”, “the app”, “we”) is an invoicing web app that runs entirely in your web browser at https://karolpasko.github.io/VSO/. It lets you create invoices, manage clients and prices, design your invoice layout, create PDF invoices and email them to your clients. The source code is public at github.com/KarolPasko/VSO, so anyone can check exactly what it does.

2. Information you enter in the app

To create invoices you may enter:

  • your business details (name, address, email, phone, website, tax/VAT number, logo, bank or payment details);
  • your clients' details (name, contact person, email address, phone number, postal address, tax/VAT number, private notes);
  • invoices and line items (descriptions, quantities, prices, dates, notes and terms), and invoices you import from your own PDF or CSV files.

Where it is stored: in your browser's local storage on the device you are using, and — only if you turn on iCloud Sync — in your own private iCloud database (see section 4). It is never uploaded to us or to any server we operate — we do not operate any. Imported PDF and CSV files are read on your device and are not uploaded anywhere.

How it is used: only to show your data in the app and to produce your invoices and PDFs.

Who can see it: only people with access to your device. We cannot see, access or recover it.

Backups: if you use “Back up everything”, a backup file is saved wherever you choose (for example the Files app or iCloud Drive). That file is then handled under the terms of the storage service you chose.

3. Google user data (optional Gmail sending)

Connecting Gmail is optional. You only need it if you want Tally to send invoices from your own Gmail address. If you connect it, you sign in through Google's own sign-in page and choose what to allow.

What Tally asks Google for

Permission (scope)Why Tally needs it
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send
“Send email on your behalf”		To send an invoice email, with the invoice PDF attached, from your Gmail account to the recipient you enter — only when you tap Send with Gmail or Send test email.
openid, email
Your Google account email address		To show which Gmail account is connected (“Connected as …”) and to send a test email to yourself.

What Tally accesses

  • Your Google account email address.
  • A temporary access token from Google that allows sending email.

Tally cannot and does not read, search, download, delete or modify any email in your mailbox, and does not access your contacts, calendar, Drive or any other Google data. The gmail.send permission does not allow reading email.

How Tally uses Google user data

  • The email you send (recipient, subject, message and attached invoice PDF) is built on your device and sent directly from your device to Google's Gmail API. It does not pass through any other server.
  • Emails are sent only when you tap a send button. Tally never sends email automatically, in the background or to anyone you did not enter.
  • Google user data is used only to provide this sending feature to you. It is not used for advertising, profiling, analytics, or to develop, improve or train any artificial intelligence or machine-learning models.

Storage and retention

  • The access token is kept only in your browser's session storage on your device and expires after about one hour. It is never sent to us or stored anywhere else.
  • Your connected email address is stored in the app's local storage on your device so the app can show the connection status. It is removed when you tap Disconnect or erase the app's data.
  • Copies of emails you send are kept by Google in your Gmail “Sent” folder, under Google's own privacy policy.

Sharing

We do not share, sell, rent or transfer Google user data to anyone. No person (including the developer) can read it, because it never leaves your device except when it is sent to Google to deliver your email.

Limited Use

Tally Invoices' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can disconnect at any time with Settings → Send from Gmail → Disconnect, which also revokes the token with Google. You can also remove Tally's access from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

4. iCloud Sync (optional)

If you turn on iCloud Sync and sign in with your Apple ID, a copy of your Tally data (business details, clients, invoices, price book, layout and settings) is stored in the private database of your iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit service, so your other devices signed in with the same Apple ID can download it. Only your Apple ID can access your private database — the developer cannot read it. Storage and processing by Apple are covered by the Apple Privacy Policy. Apple's CloudKit script is loaded from cdn.apple-cloudkit.com only when iCloud Sync is set up.

If you use a sync file instead, the file is saved wherever you choose (for example iCloud Drive) and handled under that service's terms.

To stop syncing, sign out on the iCloud Sync screen. To delete the synced copy, use More → Erase all data while signed in (the erase syncs to iCloud), or manage your iCloud data in your Apple ID settings.

5. Third-party services

  • GitHub Pages hosts the app's files. When your browser downloads the app, GitHub may process technical information such as your IP address under the GitHub Privacy Statement. We do not receive this information.
  • Google provides sign-in and the Gmail API, used only if you connect Gmail. Your use of them is covered by the Google Privacy Policy. Google's sign-in script is loaded from accounts.google.com only when you use the Gmail feature.
  • Mail apps and the share sheet: if you choose “Share PDF” or “Open in Mail”, the PDF or message is handed to the app you pick on your device, under that app's own policies.

The app contains no advertising or analytics code and sets no cookies.

6. Deleting your data

Because all data is stored on your device, you are fully in control of it:

  • Delete individual invoices, clients or items in the app.
  • Use More → Erase all data to delete everything the app has stored on the device.
  • Clearing your browser's website data for this site, or removing the app from your Home Screen, also deletes it.

We hold no copy of your data, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side. If iCloud Sync is on, the copy in your iCloud is removed as described in section 4.

7. Security

All connections to the app and to Google use HTTPS encryption. Google access tokens are short-lived and limited to sending email. Your data's security on the device depends on your device's own protection, so we recommend keeping a passcode on your device.

8. Children

Tally is a business tool and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date. Significant changes to how Google user data is used will be made only after asking for your consent again.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be raised at github.com/KarolPasko/VSO/issues.
